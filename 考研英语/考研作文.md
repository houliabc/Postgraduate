# 小作文

主要考书信和告示，纪要基本没有

## 书信

242法则，只有中间4句根据新建类别区分。**邀请推荐回复信**

### 格式

```txt
To Whorm It May Concern/Dear xx,
	自我介绍/寒暄+目的
	原因，分点作答
	感谢+期待
																								Yours sincerely,
																								Li Ming
```

### 第一段2——通用

#### 不熟

1. **As a senior student in our University/company, I'm reaching out/penning this letter to do 目的**



#### 熟人

1. **Knowing that 背景,  I'm reaching out/penning this letter to do 目的**

#### 目的

**邀请：extend an invitation to you to participate in + an online meeting/the graduation ceremony.**

**推荐信： strongly recommend a book/sb.**

**回复：reply to your questions.**

**纯建议信**：give you some advice/suggestions on your tour/improving xx.

欢迎信：extend my cordial welcome to you.

### 第二段4——区分

#### 推荐-回复-建议

1. **Some aspects of my advice/suggestions are as below.**
2. **First of all, it will be more practical（可行的）/efficient/fruitful/convenient for you to 【好处1】if you can 【建议1】**
3. **Besides，【建议2】 can make a difference to your study efficiency （人/物）【好处2】**
4. **Last but not least, 【建议3】 is necessary in order to 【好处3】**



#### 邀请

1. **To ensure your smooth participation/visit, I have prepared specific arrangements for you.**
2. **活动名 is planned to be held at/in 地点 on/at 时间**
3. **The whole activity/party will last xx hours, which includes some parts such as A, B and C**
4. （可替换2或3）Please check your schedule/ submit the application form in advance so you can attend the event on time.
5. **A detailed schedule of the event is attached for you in the attachment.**



### 第三段2——通用

1. **感谢：It would mean a great deal to me if you could accept my 【信件类型】**
2. 期待回复：I am looking forward to your early reply/hearing from you.
